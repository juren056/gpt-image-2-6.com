## Support Channels

Contact us through [support tickets](/settings/tickets) and include your account and issue details.

## What to Include

- **Account help:** your account email and a description of the issue. Never send your password or full payment card number.
- **Billing or refunds:** your order number, invoice number, charge date, and the reason for the request.
- **Generation issues:** the local task ID, approximate time, model, and a screenshot if available. Do not resend sensitive prompts unless necessary.
- **Privacy requests:** state whether you want to access, correct, export, or delete personal data and provide enough information for identity verification.
- **Copyright or abuse reports:** identify the content, explain your rights or concern, and provide a way for us to follow up.

Signed-in users may also open a [support ticket](/settings/tickets), which keeps account and technical information together.

## Response Times

We aim to acknowledge messages within 2 business days. Billing, privacy, security, and abuse reports may require additional verification and normally receive a substantive response within 5 business days. These are service targets, not guaranteed deadlines.

## Security

Do not email passwords, authentication codes, secret API keys, private keys, or full card details. AI Image support will never ask for your password.

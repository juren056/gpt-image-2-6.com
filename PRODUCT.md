# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Product Purpose

An independent image creation and editing subscription service. Users choose a scenario, prepare a prompt or references, generate, refine, save and return to their work.

## Users

The owner explicitly requests coverage of all scenarios: product images, people and characters, social covers, posters and lettering, scientific diagrams, and logos/design assets.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Preserve TanStack Start, existing accounts and credits, and fal image integrations. Public pages are statically generated on Cloudflare Free; authenticated APIs remain dynamic. Do not enable unaccepted models or pretend a disabled model is a different one. Payments must accurately reflect configuration readiness. Production schema changes require review. No schema change is planned for this upgrade.

## Brand Commitments

Preserve the approved transparent Pixel Peel banana mark. The owner requests original layouts, interactions, copy and assets, while learning useful workflows from competing services.

## Evidence on Hand

The repository contains a workbench, projects, asset collections, credit ledger, subscriptions, referrals, and analytics utilities. Their presence is not proof that every live path works; verify affected flows before release.

# Krea public workspace observation

Observed 2026-10-10 with CUA live browser at https://www.krea.ai/ and its visible Image link, https://www.krea.ai/image.

- Persistent black sidebar around 250px wide, 36px navigation rows, 10px radii, neutral dark content canvas. Suisse Intl type; controls generally 14px. Sign-in stays at the sidebar bottom.
- Search opens a 640px dialog, 24px radius. Typing `edit` filters tools; Escape dismisses. Keyboard guidance is visible.
- Image workspace is public before login. Four example cards sit above a rounded prompt composer. Clicking the lobster example fills the textbox with that exact prompt without generating.
- Model button opens a 1152 × 648px dialog at the observed desktop viewport, with three columns of roughly 310px cards and 24px gaps. Cards show previews, descriptions, cost estimates, selection and favorite affordances. Fast, other and legacy model groups are visible. Favoriting/comparison were not tested.
- Advanced settings toggles intensity/complexity/movement sliders, creativity radios and random mode; Reset is disabled at defaults. These are model-specific controls and are not added to our backend without support.
- Aspect button click did not reveal a verified changed state; no claim is made about that menu. No generation, upload, login or payment performed.
- Screenshot: `.artifacts/competitors/krea.ai/workspace-desktop.jpg`. No competitor image is shipped in our app.
